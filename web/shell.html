Как играть · How to play

Four players, 32 cards, eight each. Follow suit if you can; the highest card of the suit led takes the trick. Each player in turn, once they have seen their cards, chooses one of the 14 games none of them has played yet: seven «не брать» (don't take: tricks, hearts, the J and K «мальчики», the queens «девочки», the last two tricks, the king of hearts, or all of them at once) and the same seven «брать» (take them). 56 deals in all.

← → choose a card, Space play it (or click the card). In the game grid ↑ ↓ ← → and Space, or a click. Space or a click after each trick. Esc back to the title, F11 full screen.
